# Mantis Ai

Find B2B and local leads by describing your customer.

Mantis Ai is AI lead generation for B2B companies, startups, MSMEs and agencies, built in Gurugram. Instead of filter trees, you describe your customer in plain words, for example "clinics in Gurgaon that don't have a website" or funded SaaS startups. Mantis shows the scope and a credit quote before anything runs, then searches live across map listings, company websites, web search, funding news and public developer profiles. You get a ranked list where every lead shows why it matched and how to reach it.
